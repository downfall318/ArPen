ArPen cartridge vs. armor lab

Velocity sampled every 50 m/s. Severity: S = 1 − (1 − Senergy)(1 − D)w with D = min(B/T, 1). At w = 0 this is pure energy.
Durability: ceramic uses one remaining-HP damage curve; a full-severity sequence follows 1.00 → 0.75 → 0.25 → destroyed. Each projectile's severity is calculated against maximum HP and does not rise merely because a previous, different threat damaged the plate. Ceramic/polymer effective Krupp is keff = k₀√(HP/HPmax). Metal keeps full Krupp and loses HP from wound-channel plus compromised-dent volume, reaching 0 HP at 25% panel-volume loss.
Live overrides (applied on plot)
Velocity and base damage are vanilla cartridge inputs. Mass, calibre, penetration multiplier, threat metadata, armor physics, and stopped-hit multipliers are ArPen data. Stopped-hit health/shock values are final after the selected body-zone multiplier and do not use DayZ GlobalArmor. Penetrating-hit health/shock values are handled by the DayZ engine and are shown as engine passthrough.
Enroll new cartridge
Enrollment is session-local. Head/brain stopped hits use the head health and shock multipliers; torso and limb stopped hits use the torso multipliers. Neither alters armor damage.
Enroll new armor (all fields)
Ceramic/polymer durability uses remaining HP. Metal uses cumulative wound-channel plus compromised-dent volume: 100% HP is intact and 0% HP means 25% of original panel volume is lost/compromised. Metal Krupp never falls; accumulated dent volume reduces effective thickness across the configured surface area.
Stopped Penetrated Dots = threat level ✕ Destroyed this hit ✕ Already destroyed
